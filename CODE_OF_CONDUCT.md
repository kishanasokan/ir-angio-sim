# Code of conduct

This project brings together clinicians, trainees and developers. We want everyone to be able to contribute, ask questions and disagree productively.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good intent. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Be precise about clinical claims: cite sources, and say when something is an estimate or a guess.
- Welcome newcomers and explain context they may not have, whether clinical or technical.
- Respect patient privacy: never post patient images, records or identifiable details, even if de-identified elsewhere.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults or personal attacks.
- Sharing other people's private information without consent.
- Posting content that misrepresents the simulator as medical advice or as a validated training or certification tool.

## Enforcement

The maintainer (@kishanasokan) may edit or remove contributions and comments that break this code, and may block contributors who repeatedly do so. To report a concern privately, use the contact on the maintainer's GitHub profile.
